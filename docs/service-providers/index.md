You run a service, such as an HPC cluster or storage, and offer it to projects through the portal.
In the portal, your organisation is a *service provider* and each service is an *offering*.
Your portal role is *service provider owner*.
REANNZ staff often act on your behalf.

Follow these steps in order. Each one builds on the last.

## Your onboarding path

1. **[Tour the HPC Portal](../getting-started/portal-overview.md)** – 10 minutes.
   Log in and find your roles.
2. **[How the HPC Portal works](../getting-started/how-the-portal-works.md)**.
   Learn how offerings, orders and resources fit together.
3. **[Add an offering to the marketplace](add-offering.md)**.
   Publish your service so projects can order it.
4. **[Approve orders](approve-orders.md)**.
   Review new resources, limit changes and terminations.
5. **[Manage resources](manage-resources.md)**.
   Report usage, handle end dates and fix stuck resources.
