You can reach the REANNZ Support Team in two ways:

- **Email.** [Contact our Support Team](mailto:support@nesi.org.nz?subject=HPC%20Portal%20Request).
  The address is also under **Support** in the portal footer.
- **A support request in the portal.** Your request goes to the REANNZ service desk.
  You can follow it up in the portal.

Both reach the same team. Use whichever suits you.
Raise a request from an allocation's page when the problem is about that allocation.
The portal then links your request to the allocation, its project and its organisation.

Any project member, project manager or PI can raise a request.

## Raise a support request

1. Log in to [https://hpc-portal.reannz.co.nz](https://hpc-portal.reannz.co.nz) with **Sign in with Tuakiri**.
2. Click your name at the top right, then **User dashboard**.
3. Click **Support** at the top right of your profile card.
   The list of support requests opens.
4. Click **Create**. The **Create support request** dialog opens.
5. Leave **Request type** as **Emailed request**.
6. Say what the request is about. Either:
    - pick the **Organization**, **Project** and **Affected resource**; or
    - switch on **Issue is general and not tied to any specific organization, project, or resource**.

    **Next** stays greyed out until you do one of these.
7. Click **Next**.
8. Enter a **Title** and a **Request description**.
   See [what to include](#what-to-include).
9. Optionally, attach a screenshot under **Attachments**.
10. Click **Create**.

## Raise a request about an allocation

1. Open the resource: go to **Projects**, open your project, click the **Resources** tab and select the allocation.
2. Click the **Support** tab.
3. Click **Create**.
   The dialog fills in the **Organization**, **Project** and **Affected resource** for you.
4. Carry on from step 7 of [Raise a support request](#raise-a-support-request).

The allocation's **Support** tab lists the requests raised about it.

## Follow up a request

Open your support requests from **User dashboard** > **Support**.
Each row shows the request key, its **Status** and its **Title**.
Click the key to open the request.

On the request page you can:

- read the description and the details the portal added;
- add a comment under **Comments**;
- add a file under **Attachments**.

Replies from the Support Team appear under **Comments**.
When we close the request, its **Status** changes to **Resolved**.

## What to include

Give us enough to act without asking you again:

- your project code, for example `bsi0NNNN`;
- the allocation name, if the problem is about one;
- the order, if it is about a request you made, and its state, for example **Erred**;
- what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead;
- the exact error text, or a screenshot.

A request raised from an allocation's page already carries the project and allocation.
