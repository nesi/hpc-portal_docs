You work on a project that someone else leads.
Follow these steps in order. Each one builds on the last.

## Your onboarding path

1. **[Tour the HPC Portal](../getting-started/portal-overview.md)** – 10 minutes.
   Log in and find your roles, projects and resources.
2. **[Accept a project invitation](accept-invitation.md)**.
   Join the project your project lead invited you to.
3. **[Access Mahuika](access-mahuika.md)**.
   Start using the project's allocation.

You are done when your project shows under **Projects** and you can log in to Mahuika.

## Good to know

- Only your project manager can change limits or end dates. Ask them.
- [How the HPC Portal works](../getting-started/how-the-portal-works.md) explains projects, resources and roles.
- [Portal reference](../getting-started/portal-reference.md) lists every tab and field.
