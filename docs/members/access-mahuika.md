The HPC Portal manages your projects and allocations.
You run your work on Mahuika itself, through OnDemand or SSH.

## Before you start

- You need a role on a project that holds a Mahuika allocation.
  See [Accept a project invitation](accept-invitation.md).
- You do not apply for an HPC account.
  It is created for you when you join a project with an allocation.
  This can take a short while.
- The first time you log in to Mahuika, you set up a second login factor.
  See [First Time Login](https://docs.nesi.org.nz/Getting_Started/Accessing_the_HPCs/First_Time_Login/).

## Connect with OnDemand or Jupyter

OnDemand runs in your web browser. It is the easiest way to start.

1. Go to [https://ondemand.nesi.org.nz](https://ondemand.nesi.org.nz).
2. Log in with your institution account through Tuakiri.
3. Pick an app, for example **JupyterLab**, RStudio or a virtual desktop.

See the [OnDemand overview](https://docs.nesi.org.nz/Interactive_Computing/OnDemand/OnDemand_Overview/)
and the [JupyterLab guide](https://docs.nesi.org.nz/Interactive_Computing/OnDemand/Apps/JupyterLab/JupyterLab_Overview/).

## Connect with SSH

To use a terminal, set up SSH once.
Follow [Standard Terminal Setup](https://docs.nesi.org.nz/Getting_Started/Accessing_the_HPCs/Standard_Terminal_Setup/).
If you cannot connect, see [Login Troubleshooting](https://docs.nesi.org.nz/Getting_Started/Accessing_the_HPCs/Login_Troubleshooting/).

For other ways to connect, see
[Connecting to the Cluster](https://docs.nesi.org.nz/Getting_Started/Accessing_the_HPCs/Connecting_to_the_Cluster/).

## Get help

- Questions about your project, its members or its allocation: ask your project manager.
- Problems with Mahuika or the portal:
  [Contact our Support Team](mailto:support@nesi.org.nz?subject=HPC%20Portal%20Request).
- Outages and maintenance: see [System Status](https://docs.nesi.org.nz/Getting_Started/Getting_Help/System_Status/).

For more ways to get help, see [Contacting Support](https://docs.nesi.org.nz/Getting_Started/Getting_Help/Contacting_Support/).
