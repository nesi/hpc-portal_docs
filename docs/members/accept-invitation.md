A project manager adds you to their project by sending an invitation to your email address.
Accept it to join the project in the [REANNZ HPC Portal](https://hpc-portal.reannz.co.nz).

## Sign in with your institution account

1. Open the link in the invitation email.
2. If you are not logged in, the portal asks you to sign in.
   Click **Sign in with Tuakiri** and log in with your institution account.
   See [Log in](../getting-started/portal-overview.md#log-in).
3. If the portal asks you to complete your profile, do that first.
   Then open the invitation link again.

Log in with the account that matches the email address the invitation was sent to.
If the portal shows **Attention!** with two different email addresses,
the invitation was sent to another address.
Ask your project manager to invite the address you log in with.

## Accept the invitation

1. The **Invitation confirmation** dialog names the project and the role you are invited to.
2. Click **Accept invitation**.

The portal shows **Your invitation was accepted.** and opens the project.

Invitations expire.
If the portal says the invitation is not valid, or that it is in an expired state,
ask your project manager to send it again.
To turn down an invitation, tell your project manager.

## Find your project

1. Click **Projects** in the sidebar.
2. Find the project card and click **Details**.

The **Resources** tab shows the project's allocations.
The **Team** tab shows who else is in the project.
See [See your projects](../getting-started/portal-overview.md#see-your-projects).
