## Release Update - 10 November 2026

## New and Improved

- The portal now runs Waldur 8.1.4, the final 8.1.4 release.
- New **REANNZ allocations** page that lists all your allocations by category, with usage against limits. See Check allocation usage for [project managers](../project-managers/allocation-usage.md) and for [institution admins](../institution-admins/allocation-usage.md).

If you have any questions about any of the improvements or fixes,
please [contact our Support Team](mailto:support@nesi.org.nz?subject=HPC%20Portal%20release%20notes).
