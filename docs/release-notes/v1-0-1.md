## Release Update - 5 October 2026

## New and Improved

- The REANNZ HPC Portal is live at [hpc-portal.reannz.co.nz](https://hpc-portal.reannz.co.nz/).
- Emails from the portal carry REANNZ branding and the REANNZ logo.
- New Terms of Service, Privacy Policy and About pages.
- The AI Assistant is available in the portal.

If you have any questions about any of the improvements or fixes,
please [contact our Support Team](mailto:support@nesi.org.nz?subject=HPC%20Portal%20release%20notes).
