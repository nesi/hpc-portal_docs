## Release Update - 17 November 2026

## New and Improved

- Announcements of scheduled maintenance now appear in the portal a week (5 working days) before the maintenance starts, instead of one hour before.

## Fixes

- The sidebar shortcut to the REANNZ allocations page is now called **Admin Review**.
- A **Feedback** shortcut in the sidebar opens the REANNZ support portal, so you can tell us what you think of the HPC Portal.

If you have any questions about any of the improvements or fixes,
please [contact our Support Team](mailto:support@nesi.org.nz?subject=HPC%20Portal%20release%20notes).
