You lead a project as its project manager.
Follow these steps in order. Each one builds on the last.

<!-- TODO: add hands-on practice tutorials (order, invite) once a staging portal exists. -->

## Learn the portal

1. **[Tour the HPC Portal](../getting-started/portal-overview.md)** – 10 minutes.
   Log in and find your roles, projects and resources.
2. **[How the HPC Portal works](../getting-started/how-the-portal-works.md)** – 10 minutes.
   Learn how projects, orders and approvals fit together before you request anything.

## Set up your project

1. **[Request a new project](request-new-project.md)** and order an allocation.
2. **[Track your order](track-order.md)** until the resource shows **OK**.
3. **[Invite your team](invite-team.md)**.

You are set up when your resource shows **OK** and your team is under **Team** → **Active**.

## Later, when you need to

- **[Check allocation usage](allocation-usage.md)** across all your allocations.
- **[Change allocation limits](change-limits.md)** for more compute or storage.
- **[Extend an end date](extend-request.md)** before the allocation ends.

Look up roles and screens in the [Portal reference](../getting-started/portal-reference.md).
