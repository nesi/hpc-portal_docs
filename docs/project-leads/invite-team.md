As a project manager, you decide who is on your project.
You manage the team from the project's **Team** tab.

## Project roles

| Role | Give it to | What they can do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Project member | Researchers who use the allocation | View the project and its resources. |
| Project manager | You and any co-leads | Manage the team, change limits and request end date changes. |

A project can have more than one project manager.
See [What each role can do](../getting-started/portal-overview.md#what-each-role-can-do).

## Send an invitation

Invite people by email. They accept with their institution login.

1. Open your project and click the **Team** tab.
2. Click **Add**, then **Invite by mail**.
3. In the **Invite by email** dialog, enter the person's **Email** and choose their **Role**.
   To invite more people at once, click **Add another user**.
4. Click **Continue**.
5. Add a **Custom message** if you wish, then click **Send invitation**.

Use the email address the person logs in to the portal with.
They get an email with a link to accept.
Send them [Accept a project invitation](../members/accept-invitation.md).

### Add someone already in the portal

If the person already belongs to a project in your organisation, you can add them directly:

1. On the **Team** tab, click **Add**, then **Member**.
2. In the **Add user** dialog, find the person under **User**.
3. Choose their **Role**. To make the role end on a set date, fill in **Role expires on**.
4. Click **Add role**.

The portal does not email them, so let them know.

### Follow up an invitation

The **Invitations** list on the **Team** tab shows each invitation's **Status** and **Expires at** date.
Click the **⋮** menu on a row:

- **Resend** sends the invitation again, for example once it has expired.
- **Cancel** withdraws a pending invitation.

## Change a role

1. On the **Team** tab, in the **Active** list, click the **⋮** menu on the person's row.
2. Click **Edit**.
3. In the **Edit project member** dialog, change the **Role** or **Role expires on**.
4. Click **Save**.

## Remove a member

1. On the **Team** tab, in the **Active** list, click the **⋮** menu on the person's row.
2. Click **Remove**.
3. Confirm.

The portal shows **Team member has been removed.**
The person no longer has a role on the project in the portal.

To remove several people at once, select their rows and click **Remove**.
