The [REANNZ HPC Portal](https://hpc-portal.reannz.co.nz/) is where you manage
HPC projects, team members and allocations on Mahuika and Freezer.

!!! warning "Who can use the HPC Portal"
    At present only members of Public Research Organisations have access.
    Everyone else should keep using [my.nesi.org.nz](https://my.nesi.org.nz).
    See [Logging in to my.nesi.org.nz](https://docs.nesi.org.nz/Getting_Started/my-nesi-org-nz/Logging_in_to_my-nesi-org-nz/).

## Find your guide

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

- **Project members**

    ---

    You work on a project someone else leads.
    Sign in, accept an invitation and reach Mahuika.

    [Accept an invitation](members/accept-invitation.md)

- **Project leads**

    ---

    You lead a project.
    Request projects and allocations, invite your team and extend end dates.

    [Request a new project](project-leads/request-new-project.md)

- **Institution admins**

    ---

    You manage HPC access for your institution.
    Approve orders and end dates, and create projects.

    [Approve requests](institution-admins/approve-requests.md)

</div>
